# Reference notes (captured 2026-10-11)

Jesse's three references. One line each: what is worth stealing, and what is not.

- **01 Apple iPhone Duo** (`refero/`, `apple-duo/`). Refero style sheet
  https://styles.refero.design/style/a73148b9-449b-42cd-9f38-86ef694f500e, live page https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo.
  White gallery, one huge object centred per screen, tight black two-line headlines ("Hello, hello.", "Think vast."),
  pale grey bands holding white cards, 28 px media frames, no shadows. Steal the canvas, the type discipline and the
  one-object-per-screen rule. Do not steal the blue or the product photography.
- **02 Jeton** (`jeton2/`). Mobbin link (login wall) https://mobbin.com/sites/jeton-e806debf-7f7d-4259-8a64-d62b53f2bc10/e8c0e585-9804-4430-a23b-3f768ed5ceec/preview,
  live site https://www.jeton.com. One flat coral field edge to edge, a single abstract 3D form (stacked discs that
  turn into a coil) moving slowly behind 106 px white type, headline bottom-left, action bottom-right, almost no
  chrome. Steal the single colour flood, the abstract object and the type scale. Do not steal the red.
  **Seen only in part:** the site was in scheduled maintenance (9 to 12 Oct 2026) and would not scroll. The first
  screen is from pixels; the lower sections are known from text and heading sizes only (`jeton/page.json`).
- **03 Shopify Editions** (`shopify-editions/`, `shopify-spring2026/`). Mobbin link (login wall)
  https://mobbin.com/sites/shopify-editions-9e30e61f-2e6b-463f-827b-71f7d6ba7c2e/4a00a721-bc61-4e94-9d3f-6677f0d0614d/preview,
  live https://www.shopify.com/editions and https://www.shopify.com/editions/spring2026. Index: a pale gallery wall
  with each edition as a 3D object on a shelf. Spring '26: every chapter opens on a full-screen scene with a single
  word at 120 px ("Agentic", "Sidekick", "Online", "Finance", "Developer"), then product cards. Steal the chapter
  opener. Do not steal the dark detail sections or the particle forests.
